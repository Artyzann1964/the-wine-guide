- Base: Corinthia Budapest, Erzsébet körút 43–49, Pest.
- No hire car: central Pest choices are planned on foot or using metro/tram; Buda reservations and late returns use taxis.
- Confirmed booking: **FELIX Kitchen & Bar, Thursday 3 September 2026 at 19:30**.
- Recommended departure for FELIX: ask the Corinthia to call a taxi for approximately **18:55–19:00**. The taxi route is about 3.9 km; traffic time is variable.

## Best shortlists

### Breakfast and coffee

1. **Cirkusz** — best complete breakfast within a nine-minute walk; go before 09:00 and order a Benedict plus Bagira coffee.
2. **VAJ · Sas** — best pastry-first breakfast; choose a plain laminated pastry, then a sourdough sandwich only if it replaces lunch.
3. **Espresso Embassy** — best coffee-first stop; ask what is best as espresso and filter rather than ordering by habit.
4. **TATI** — best weekend brunch near the hotel; farm omelette, seasonal vegetables and dry Hungarian sparkling.
5. **Gerlóczy Café** — best quieter old-Budapest breakfast; terrace if the weather works.
6. **New York Café** — go once for the room, ideally near 07:00; treat the premium as admission and keep the order focused.

### Lunch and informal dinner

1. **Stand25** — best serious Hungarian cooking without committing to a top-end tasting menu.
2. **Gettó Gulyás** — best direct, good-value introduction to gulyás, pörkölt and dumplings.
3. **Retek Bisztró** — best complete traditional set: goulash, duck or paprikash, then Somlói.
4. **Két Szerecsen** — best all-day neighbourhood bistro; the 19 August–8 September chef offer covers the trip.
5. **N28 Wine & Kitchen** — best wine-led bistro; ask for a volcanic white, Kékfrankos and one richer red.
6. **Rosenstein** — best Jewish-Hungarian family cooking and a worthwhile traditional meal near Keleti.

### Special dinners

1. **FELIX** — the confirmed Thursday evening: Josper cooking, historic Buda room and deep cellar.
2. **Stand Restaurant** — two-Michelin-star benchmark; current menu 89,500 Ft (about £210), before 15% service.
3. **Borkonyha Winekitchen** — wine-integrated Michelin dining with a notably deep Hungarian list.
4. **SALT Budapest** — modern Hungarian tasting rooted in preservation, herbs and farmhouse ideas.
5. **Essência** — Portuguese-Hungarian fine dining; lunch gives à la carte control, dinner is tasting-menu only.
6. **Babel** — the most conceptual Carpathian tasting; the eight-course route is 59,000 Ft (about £139), before service.

### Wine, beer and cocktails

- **Kadarka** for an inexpensive, close-to-hotel Hungarian glass.
- **Tasting Table** for a guided introduction to Hungarian regions.
- **VinoPest** for a sommelier-led flight and bottle to take home.
- **DOBLO** for Hungarian wine, boards and live music in a brick cellar.
- **Hopaholic** for a compact, constantly changing craft-beer list six minutes away.
- **Élesztőház** for a full beer-led evening across thirty taps and a courtyard.
- **High Note SkyBar** for a sunset view and Hungarian sparkling; reserve the normal terrace, not a high-minimum Panorama Tower.
- **MÚZSA** for the most beautiful grand-hotel nightcap and live music Thursday–Saturday.
- **Boutiq’Bar** for bartender-led creative cocktails; state spirit, flavour direction and sweetness limit.
- **Black Swan** for a reserved, immersive speakeasy experience eight minutes from the hotel.
- **Blue Fox** for published-price signatures and weekend music without nightclub volume.

### Night out

- **A38 Ship** is the strongest event-led night because its current September programme is published. Buy the event ticket first and plan the taxi or tram.
- **Ötkert** is the mainstream late club: two rooms, late doors, simple drinks and taxi home.
- **Szimpla Kert** is the essential ruin-bar visit: go early, explore the building, have one beer or fröccs, then move on.

## Price and menu discipline

- Exact prices appear only when a current official web menu or valid menu document supports them.
- Stable pricing is not invented for coffee counters, changing natural-wine lists, experimental cocktail bars or clubs. Those entries use a clearly marked **planning estimate**.
- Service is commonly 12–15%; the guide calls this out where the official menu does. Always check whether it is already on the bill before adding another tip.
- GBP figures are planning conversions, not card-settlement quotes. A card issuer's spread may change the final amount.
- N28's old food and wine PDF URLs now return error/HTML pages. They were deliberately removed and replaced with the current official seasonal pages.
- Stand's current 32-page wine-list PDF was downloaded, text-checked and visually rendered before inclusion.

## Image and source discipline

- Every Budapest card has a venue-specific photograph.
- Official photography is preferred. Where an official host blocks reliable delivery, a clearly labelled Michelin, city-guide or established editorial venue image is used.
- Logos, bottle labels, generic food stock, guessed WordPress filenames and non-venue stand-ins are not used.
- The 15 new image assets were downloaded as binary images and visually checked. Weak Két Szerecsen, Babel and Stand candidates were rejected and replaced before release.
